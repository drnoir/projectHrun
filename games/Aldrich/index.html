About Flixlstix

Our Story

Flixlstix Development Studios is an indie game team driven by imagination, chaos, and pure creative energy. We specialize in crafting pixel-rich, emotionally immersive games that blend classic mechanics with modern storytelling. Every project we take on — from the art to the lore — is built with attention to detail and a rebellious spirit. We're not here to follow formulas; we’re here to build worlds you remember. HRUN is our boldest adventure yet — and we’re just getting started.
